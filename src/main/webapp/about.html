Cultivating Connection Between City & Soil

AgroTravel was founded to revitalize sustainable rural livelihoods, protect biodiversity, and give families and conscious travelers genuine, unhurried agro-ecological experiences.

Our Core Purpose

The AgroTravel Mission

We envision a world where rural farmers thrive through agricultural diversification and urban citizens understand where their food truly comes from.

🚜

Farm Tourism

Inviting visitors directly onto working farms for educational estate tours, tractor rides, canal walks, and rural homestays.

🌱

Sustainable Agriculture

Championing chemical-free organic farming, water conservation, permaculture raised-beds, and indigenous cow breeding.

🧺

Direct Farm Products

Eliminating middlemen so that 100% pure raw honey, heirloom rice, and crisp vegetables reach consumers at honest prices.

Comprehensive Offerings

What AgroTravel Provides

A unified platform for tourism exploration, experiential learning, and farm-fresh commerce.

🌾 Curated Farm Tours

Detailed guides to verified agro-estates in Salem, Coimbatore, Pollachi, and Ooty Foothills with full amenity details.

📅 Hassle-Free Bookings

Seamless reservation system backed by Java Servlets and MySQL for instant registration of day visits and group retreats.

🍯 Fresh Farm Marketplace

Session-driven shopping cart and direct checkout for harvesting organic honey, traditional rice, fruits, and veggies.

📜 Transparent Reviews

Multi-tier feedback engine utilizing XML data storage, XSD schema validation, XSLT summaries, and XPath filtering.
